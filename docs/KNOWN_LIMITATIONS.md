# Known limitations

- W.D. performed both the repeat VHR interpretation and internal resolution; this is not independent validation.
- Five records remain unresolved after internal resolution, and 92 uncertain or uninterpretable records are excluded from the 525-record working set.
- The working set retains only 16 rubber records; rubber results are exploratory and complete four-fold q25 performance is not estimable.
- The 622-record branch uses original labels and is retained as a sensitivity benchmark, not as gold-standard ground truth.
- q25 performance combines validation distance, reduced training support, class-support loss, unequal folds, and one realised spatial partition.
- The sample is not probability based, so design-unbiased map accuracy is not claimed.
